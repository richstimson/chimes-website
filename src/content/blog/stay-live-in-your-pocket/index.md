Until now, Chimes only shared a van's location while the app was open on the screen. Put the
phone in your pocket, or let it lock, and your van stopped moving on the map. Customers down the
road never got their chime.

That's fixed. **Since Chimes 2.2.2, your van stays Live when Chimes is in the background or your
phone is locked.** Go Live at the start of your round, put the phone away, and drive.

## What it means for you

- **Customers keep getting chimes.** As you drive into someone's area, their phone chimes, just
  as it does when Chimes is open on your screen.
- **Your phone is free for everything else.** Take card payments, answer calls, use your sat-nav
  or just lock the phone. Chimes keeps sharing in the background.
- **Parked up and serving?** Chimes still updates your position about every 10 minutes, so you
  stay on the map while you serve.

## How to use it

1. Update Chimes to the latest version from the App Store (2.2.2 or later, on iPhone or iPad).
2. Go Live as usual: open **Location Sharing** and tap **Share Location**.
3. Put your phone away. Your iPhone shows a blue location indicator at the top of the screen
   while Chimes is sharing in the background. That's iOS telling you Chimes is using your
   location, as it should.

There's no extra permission to give. The location access you already allowed is enough, as long
as you go Live from inside the app.

## You're still in control

- **Stop Sharing stops it, straight away.** When your round is over, tap **Stop Sharing** and
  Chimes stops sending your location, in the background too.
- **Closing Chimes completely stops sharing.** If you swipe Chimes away in the app switcher, your
  van stops updating. Open Chimes again and it carries on.
- **If Chimes can't share in the background**, the Location Sharing screen tells you, and how to
  fix it in Settings, rather than leaving you to guess.

## What about the battery?

We tested it on a 75-minute ride with an iPhone 13 in a pocket. Battery went down at the same
rate with the van Live as it did Offline, about 8% an hour either way. One tip: iPhone's Low Power Mode can pause apps' background location, so
on a long round keep the phone charging in the cab if you can.
